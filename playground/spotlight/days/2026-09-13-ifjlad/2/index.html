Corner

The corner is a curve now

border-radius has always controlled how big a corner is; it never let you say what shape it should be, so every squircle on the web was an SVG clip-path or a PNG. corner-shape splits the job in two — the radius still sizes the corner box, and superellipse(k) draws the curve inside it. One exponent slides continuously from a scooped bite through a circle, an iOS squircle, and on to a hard square.
