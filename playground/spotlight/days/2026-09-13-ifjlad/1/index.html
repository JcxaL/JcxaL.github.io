Stagger without counting

Staggered motion used to mean a ladder of :nth-child() rules, or a loop in JavaScript writing --i onto every node. sibling-index() and sibling-count() return an element's position and its group size as plain integers, so animation-delay: calc(sibling-index() * 60ms) is the entire mechanism — and it re-times itself the moment the list changes.
