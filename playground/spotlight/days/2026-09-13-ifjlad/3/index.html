Text box

Cutting the invisible leading

Every font reserves a sliver of vertical space above its capitals and below its baseline — half-leading. It is why a heading never optically sits where its box says it does, and why designers have shipped a decade of magic margin-top: -0.3em corrections. text-box-trim tells the browser to cut that space off the first and last line, and text-box-edge says which typographic landmark to cut back to — cap height, x-height or the font's own text edge.
