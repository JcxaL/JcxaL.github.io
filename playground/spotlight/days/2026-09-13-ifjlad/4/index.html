Prediction

Look-ahead picks how far down the predicted list to follow: 0% is the next predicted point, 100% is the furthest one the browser will commit to.

Ahead of the finger

A drag is always late. By the time a pointermove reaches your handler, is laid out and is composited, the finger has moved on — and the gap is exactly what "laggy" feels like. getPredictedEvents() hands you the browser's own extrapolation of where the pointer is heading, computed from recent velocity and trajectory, so you can render at the predicted position and let reality catch up. Move fast and the guess is worth several pixels; stop and it collapses to nothing.
