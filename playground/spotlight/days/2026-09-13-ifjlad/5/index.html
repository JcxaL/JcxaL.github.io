field-sizing has no opinion about limits — you still bound it with ordinary max-width / max-height, and it stops there.

Fields that fit their contents

The auto-growing textarea is the most re-implemented component on the web: listen for input, copy the value into a hidden mirror or read scrollHeight, write it back as a height, repeat on resize, and hope nothing jitters. field-sizing: content deletes all of it. A textarea grows and shrinks with its text, an input hugs what you typed, and a <select> resizes to the option you picked — with max-height and max-width still doing the bounding.
