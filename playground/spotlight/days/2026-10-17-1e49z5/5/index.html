When the input is missing, the declaration bows out.

All three cards are the same component, styled by the same rule: border-radius: attr(data-radius px, …). They differ only in what that attr() falls back to when the attribute isn't there. Pull the attributes off and watch two of them guess wrong — revert-rule is the one that hands the decision back to the rule underneath.
