Spring

--k-x 0.00
--k-y 0.00

The motion lives in two custom properties.

One spring loop tracks the pointer and writes --k-x and --k-y onto a region. Nothing else is scripted: every tilt, glare, depth offset and parallax layer below is a plain CSS declaration reading those two numbers. Because they're registered with @property the browser interpolates them, and because the effects use translate and rotate rather than transform, your own transforms survive.
