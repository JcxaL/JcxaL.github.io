Page setup

Sheet

Sixteen boxes around the type.

A printed page is not a scrolling box. @page gives you the sheet, its margins and sixteen margin boxes — running heads, folios, counter(page) of counter(pages) — and browsers have shipped it since Chrome 131 and Safari 18.2. These three sheets mirror the real rule below with the same custom properties, so what you drag is what prints.

Hit Print preview to see the browser lay out the real thing.
