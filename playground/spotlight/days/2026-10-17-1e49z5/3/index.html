Underline

The line goes under the descenders.

Every underline has to decide what to do when a j, a g or a Vietnamese tone mark drops below the baseline. text-decoration-skip-ink cuts holes in the line; text-underline-position moves the whole line out of the way instead. They are different answers, and for scripts with deep descenders only the second one really works.

left · right

In vertical writing modes the keyword picks a side instead. Same property, same job — keep the line off the glyphs.

The W3C's i18n group is blunt about it: under is “a little unreliable”, because where the line lands still depends on the font's own metrics, and from-font only does anything for the few fonts that ship the measurement. Test with the faces you actually ship — which is exactly what the sliders above are for.
