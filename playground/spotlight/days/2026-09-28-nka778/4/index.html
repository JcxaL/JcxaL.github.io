Interest, not hover

A hover card is three problems: enter, leave, and the gap between them. The Interest Invoker API hands all three to the browser — interestfor="id" on any element, and the two pseudo-classes :interest-source and :interest-target to style both ends. Hover, focus, or long-press: same event, no listeners. Slow down the delays below and the intent behaviour becomes obvious.
