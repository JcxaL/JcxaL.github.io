Markup that streams in — out of order

Frameworks have shipped out-of-order streaming for years: send the shell now, fill the slow slots whenever the data lands. The browsers are taking it over. A slot is marked in the HTML with <?start>/<?end> around its skeleton, and a later <template for="id"> anywhere in the stream replaces it. The JS half is the new insertion family — appendHTML(), setHTML() and the streaming streamAppendHTML(), which hands you a WritableStream. Hit stream: the slowest card resolves first on purpose.
