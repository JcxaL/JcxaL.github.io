UI Spotlight · Day 128 · 28 September 2026

Light that adds up, frames that fit, intent that waits

Five patterns pulled from what shipped and what's shipping: a canvas that finally sums light instead of summing bytes, iframes that size themselves in CSS, text strokes that stop eating their letterforms, hover cards the browser times for you, and page slots that fill out of order. Every demo is a single self-contained file — no build, no CDN, no framework — and each one feature-detects and degrades where the platform hasn't caught up yet.
