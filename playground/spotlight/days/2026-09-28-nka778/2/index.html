Iframes that hug their content

Embedding someone else's widget used to mean guessing a height, then patching it with a postMessage resize loop. Chrome 154 moves it into CSS: the parent sets frame-sizing: content-height and the embedded document opts in with <meta name="responsive-embedded-sizing">. Each card below holds a real srcdoc iframe — grow the content and watch the frames re-fit.
