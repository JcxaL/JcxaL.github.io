Type that grows

The word is rasterised once into an alpha mask. Vines are then seeded at the feet of the strokes and climb inside that mask — every step that would leave the letterform is rejected and re-aimed — so the plant can only ever grow into the shape of the type. Leaves and blooms are the one thing allowed to spill out.
