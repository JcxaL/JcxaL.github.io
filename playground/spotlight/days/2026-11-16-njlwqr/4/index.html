Shake to Summon the Shelf

Start dragging any card, then shake the pointer — six direction reversals inside 100 px and a shelf flies in under your cursor to catch the drop. Or just park against the left or right edge for 600 ms. Nothing appears while you drag normally.

Dragging works with a mouse, a trackpad or a finger. Drop onto the shelf to stack it · Esc dismisses · the stack flips to a list from its header.
