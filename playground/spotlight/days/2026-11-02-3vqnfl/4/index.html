The half second after a click

Most components animate the click and abandon the moment after it. Four rules fix that: reserve the space every state will need before any of them appear, give destructive actions a window instead of a dialog, assume every gesture can be interrupted, and make sure the information still arrives when motion is switched off.

1 · No layout jump

“Save” → “Saving…” → “Saved” are three different widths. Measure all three up front and the row beside it never moves. Turn reservation off and watch the timestamp slide.

edited 2 minutes ago

2 · A window, not a dialog

Delete acts immediately and leaves an undo window with the time visibly draining. Escape also undoes, and the row's space is held the whole time.

3 · Every gesture can be interrupted

Drag to the end to confirm. Let go early, drag off the track, or lose the pointer and it springs back — each cancellation path is logged.

4 · Optimistic, and reversible

The count moves on the click, not on the response. When the request fails the count rolls back and says so — the optimism is only worth it if the rollback is honest.
