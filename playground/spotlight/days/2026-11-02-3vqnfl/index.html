Five drops that hand the work to the platform

Today's five all move something out of application code and into the thing underneath it: a 3D scene declared as a stylesheet, masonry the browser packs itself, a number that animates per place instead of per string, the half-second of feedback most components skip, and an interface sound kit with no audio files in it.
