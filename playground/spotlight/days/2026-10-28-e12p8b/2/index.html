Centred Without a Wrapper

Vertical centring used to mean reaching for flex or grid, which meant an extra element and a new formatting context. align-content applies to block containers now — same keywords, no wrapper, and margin collapsing and text-overflow keep working. Flip to the flex equivalent and watch the spacing jump.
