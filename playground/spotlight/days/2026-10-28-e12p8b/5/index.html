A Dropzone Around the Real Input

Most upload components throw the native control away — opacity: 0, a label pretending to be a button, and keyboard users left holding the bag. ::file-selector-button restyles the browser's own button instead, so drag, paste and preview can be wrapped around a control that still focuses, still announces, still works. Everything here stays on your machine.
