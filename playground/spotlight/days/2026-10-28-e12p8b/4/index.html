Two Hands, Two Buttons

A mouse can hover, so it can be shown what a control will do before committing. A thumb cannot, so it needs the commitment itself to be deliberate. any-hover and any-pointer let one component ship both: a magnetic sheen and a single click for pointers, a press-and-hold with a visible fill for touch.
