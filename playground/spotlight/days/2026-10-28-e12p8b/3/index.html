Units Cut From the Letters

Icon-and-label alignment is usually a pixel someone nudged once at one size. cap, ex, ch and ic are measured from the font in use — cap height, x-height, the advance of 0, the advance of 水. Size the icon in 1cap and it matches the capitals at every size. Drag the type size and watch which rows stay honest.
