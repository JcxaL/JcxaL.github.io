Groups That Keep Their Clip

A card that morphs open normally lets its own contents fly outside its rounded box, because every named element animates in a flat sibling list. view-transition-group: contain nests them back inside the card's group — and ::view-transition-group-children() is the wrapper you can finally put overflow: clip on. Open a card, then flip the switch and open it again.

Controls

Containment

With contain on the card, the thumbnail and heading become children of the card's group instead of siblings of it, so the clip travels with the morph.

Nearest

nearest on a child says "nest me under whichever ancestor group is closest", which keeps the markup honest when cards get re-ordered or re-used.

Escape hatch

none pulls a child back out of the ancestor group — the flat behaviour, for the one element that genuinely needs to fly past the edge.
