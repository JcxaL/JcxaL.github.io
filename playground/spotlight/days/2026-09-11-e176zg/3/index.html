Polar controls

Trig Type

cos() and sin() turn a flat row of glyphs into a ring, an arc, a spiral or a travelling wave without a single line of layout maths in JavaScript. Script does one job here — split the word and hand each letter its index — and the stylesheet does the rest, so every letter below is positioned by the same two calc() expressions.

A · Ring translate(r·cos(a), r·sin(a))

polar

B · Arc headline sweep + start angle

C · Spiral --rr: calc(18px + i * 4.1px)

D · Wave sin(var(--phase) + i · 42deg) animated via @property
