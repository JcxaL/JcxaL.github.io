Hint Popovers

Every tooltip library eventually hits the same wall: open a menu, hover an item, and the tooltip slams the menu shut — because both were competing for one stack. popover="hint" gives hints a stack of their own. A hint never dismisses an auto popover, but it does dismiss other hints that are not its ancestors. Open both menus below and hover the rows.
