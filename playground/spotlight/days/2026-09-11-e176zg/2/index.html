Gap Rules

Dividers used to mean a border on every child, a pseudo-element, or a fake column. Gap decorations move the job to the container: grid and flex now take column-rule, and a matching row-rule paints the horizontal gaps. The lines live in the gap, so they never shift the layout and they disappear on their own when a row wraps.
