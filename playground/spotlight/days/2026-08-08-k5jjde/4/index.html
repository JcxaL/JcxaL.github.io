Drop 04 · Micro-interaction

Border Beam

A thin arc of light continuously chases each card's rounded border — pure CSS, one rotating custom property, compositor-only. No SVG stroke, no repaint per frame.

Default

6s loop, amber beam, hairline border.

Fast & wide

3s loop, cyan → violet, wider beam spread.

Slow & reversed

9s loop running counter-clockwise, thin rose beam.

Live-tuned

Drag the panel dials to reshape this one in real time.

Tune the live card

Uses @property --beam-angle, a spinning conic-gradient, and mask-composite:exclude to keep only the border ring.
