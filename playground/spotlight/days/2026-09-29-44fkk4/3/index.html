Emphasis marks

A second layer of stress

Bold and italic change the letterforms. text-emphasis leaves them alone and puts a small mark beside every character instead — the boten of East Asian typesetting, and a very usable Latin highlight. Three sub-properties: -style, -color, -position, plus a custom string if none of the built-in shapes fit.

Running text

The mark rides above each glyph and reserves its own space, so the line box grows instead of colliding.

Vertical writing

In writing-mode: vertical-rl the logical over keyword resolves to the right-hand side — which is where boten belong.

Every built-in shape

filled and open variants of each, plus an arbitrary string.

filled dot open dot circle double triangle sesame a string
It is not a decoration. Marks never overlap the glyph and are skipped on spaces and punctuation by default — text-emphasis-skip controls which.
One inherited value. The shorthand is inherited, so a mark set on a paragraph applies to every inline inside it — handy, and easy to over-apply.
Space is reserved. The line box grows to fit the marks, which is why the samples above use generous line-height.
