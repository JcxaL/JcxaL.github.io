No space before the paren. if (…) with a space is an invalid declaration — if() has to read as a function token.
First match wins. Conditions are semicolon-separated and evaluated in order; else: is the last arm, and without one an unmatched if() is guaranteed-invalid.
style() sees computed values. It queries the element's own custom properties, so inheritance does the plumbing — a container sets --variant and every descendant answers.
