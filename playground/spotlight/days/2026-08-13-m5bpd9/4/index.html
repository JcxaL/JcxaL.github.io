Interruption stress-test

Simulate failure

Committed

The "missing twenty percent" of a good interaction is what happens when it's interrupted: a click during a click, a scroll away mid-load, a toggle spammed twice. Nothing here jumps, restarts from zero, or leaves a spinner stranded.

1 · No layout jump, ever

Fixed-size button. Click it mid-flight and it settles cleanly to idle instead of snapping.

2 · Skeleton reserves real height

These rows are already their final height before content exists, so nothing shifts when it arrives.
