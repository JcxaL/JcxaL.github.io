Press, squeeze, morph

Material 3 Expressive replaced the flat press state with shape: a button’s corner radius collapses under your finger, a segmented group squeezes its neighbours aside, a FAB morphs into a bar. The motion is spring, not ease — and CSS can express a real spring with a generated linear() easing, sampled here from the bounce slider.

1 · Shape as press feedback

Hold one down: border-radius travels from 26px to 10px on the spring curve while the button squeezes to 94%.

2 · Connected group — the squeeze

The selected segment rounds out and grows; its immediate neighbours compress, so the group reads as one flexible object instead of four rectangles.

3 · FAB → bar, and a shape-morph loader

Tap the FAB. Width, height and all four corner pairs travel on the same spring, so the shape never looks like it is scaling a rectangle.
