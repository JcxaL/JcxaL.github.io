Anchor scope

Anchors that know their own card

Anchor names are globally visible, so six cards all called --card means every tooltip latches onto the last one in the DOM. anchor-scope fixes it — and it goes on the common ancestor of the anchor and the tip (here, each <li>), not on the anchor itself. Flip the switch and watch six tooltips pile onto card six.
