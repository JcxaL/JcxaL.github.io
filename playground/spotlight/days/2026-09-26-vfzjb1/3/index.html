Spacing

Air between scripts

Set Japanese next to Latin and the two collide: 「東京」 and “Tokyo” were designed on different bodies. text-autospace asks the engine to insert the typographic quarter-space between ideographs and alphanumerics — the rule Japanese typesetters have applied by hand for a century. Safari 27 adds the insert keyword; Firefox and Safari already ship the granular values.
