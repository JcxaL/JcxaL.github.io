DROP 04 · MICRO-INTERACTION

The scrollspy with
no spy

Every docs site ships the same hundred lines: an IntersectionObserver, a threshold you guessed, and a class you toggle. scroll-target-group hands that job to the browser — mark the container, keep your own <a href="#id"> markup, and the link pointing at whatever is on screen matches :target-current.

01 · A STICKY TABLE OF CONTENTS

The nav is ordinary anchors. scroll-target-group: auto on the nav is the entire wiring — scroll the panel on the right and watch the highlight follow. Nudge scroll-margin-top to change which section counts as "current".

02 · A HORIZONTAL CHIP BAR

Same mechanism turned sideways: the chips track a snapping filmstrip. Because the markers are real links, they keep their keyboard behaviour, their focus ring and their href.

03 · A DOT RAIL

Shrink the markers to dots and you have the pagination indicator from every full-screen landing page — without a single line of scroll maths.
