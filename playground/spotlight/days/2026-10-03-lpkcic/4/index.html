The button that yields

Same work, three schedulers. await scheduler.yield() parks the rest of a function in a continuation that resumes ahead of other pending tasks — so you can break up a long job without losing your place in the queue the way setTimeout(0) does. Watch the spinner and the latency meter while each runs.
