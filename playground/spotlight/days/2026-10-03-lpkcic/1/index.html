Motion on a ray

One line of CSS replaces a pile of sine and cosine: offset-path: ray(40deg closest-side) gives an element a straight path outward from its own origin, and offset-distance slides it along. No transforms to compose, no trigonometry, and contain keeps everything inside the box.

This browser doesn’t support offset-path: ray(), so the elements below will sit still.
