scroll anchoring

The feed that doesn’t jump

Content keeps arriving above where you are reading. Browsers quietly fix this with scroll anchoring: they pick an anchor node in view and adjust scrollTop to keep it still. overflow-anchor: none opts out — and you can watch the exact pixels of drift it costs.

1 · Anchoring on (default)

overflow-anchor: auto
drift 0px · inserted 0 · scrollTop 0

2 · Anchoring off

overflow-anchor: none
drift 0px · inserted 0 · scrollTop 0

3 · Late-loading media, same page

image placeholders grow
drift 0px · grown 0 ·

Scroll each feed to the middle and leave it: the green pane holds your line of text, the red one slides it away. Drift is measured against a watched message, not against scrollTop.
