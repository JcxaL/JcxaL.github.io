The Colour Decides the Ink

Theme tokens change at runtime; the label on top has to stay readable. contrast-color() takes the background you already declared and returns whichever of black or white reads better against it — so a brand colour can come from a database, a user picker or a hue slider, and nobody has to maintain a parallel map of text colours. Drag the sliders and watch the ink flip.

The declaration
