Trim the Leading

Every line of text carries invisible padding — the font's ascent above the cap height and its descent below the baseline. Designers have compensated with magic negative margins for twenty years. text-box-trim and text-box-edge cut it away at the engine level, so a heading's box ends exactly where the letters do. Firefox 154 was the last engine to ship it; the pair is Baseline now.
