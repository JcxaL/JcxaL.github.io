A Carousel With No Script

The arrows and the dots were always the reason a carousel needed JavaScript. Now the scroller generates them: ::scroll-button() makes up to four buttons that know when they've hit the end and disable themselves, ::scroll-marker gives every child a dot, and :target-current styles whichever one you're looking at. Keyboard focus and scroll position come free, because it's still just a scroller.
