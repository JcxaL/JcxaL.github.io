Rules in the Gaps

Dividers between grid and flex items used to mean border hacks on every nth child, or a pseudo-element stretched across the gutter. Gap decorations put the line where it belongs: column-rule and the new row-rule paint down the middle of the gap itself, without changing the gap's size and without knowing how many items wrapped.
