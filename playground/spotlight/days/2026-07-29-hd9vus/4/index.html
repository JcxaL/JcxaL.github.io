Vector Lasso Select

Drag a rectangle to multi-select — modeled as a vector (origin + signed magnitude) instead of width/height, so it works dragging in any direction.

Drag to select · Shift/Cmd-drag to add to selection · Click a swatch to toggle it · Esc clears · drag near the edge to auto-scroll
