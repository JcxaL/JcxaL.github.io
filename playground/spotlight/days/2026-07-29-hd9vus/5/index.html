Floating Selection Bubble Menu

Select any text below — a formatting toolbar appears right above it, flipping below when there's no room, exactly like Medium or Notion's inline editor.

Try selecting a sentence

The trick almost every clone gets wrong: toolbar buttons must bind to mousedown instead of click, and call preventDefault() — otherwise the browser collapses your text selection before the click handler ever fires, wiping out the very selection you meant to format.

This bubble listens for selectionchange on the whole document, reads range.getBoundingClientRect() to find where the selected text actually sits on screen, and positions itself centered above it — flipping below the selection automatically if there isn't enough room near the top of the viewport.

Select a phrase, then try bold, italic, highlight, a link, or use "Copy as Markdown" to grab the whole article's formatting as plain-text Markdown.
