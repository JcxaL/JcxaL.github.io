Scroll Branch
Timeline

A vertical progress line draws itself as you scroll, and each milestone card fades, unblurs and scales in from its own entry/exit window — no JavaScript scroll listener required, all native CSS.

01 · entry()

Native scroll-timeline

animation-timeline: view() ties every card's keyframes to how far it has travelled through the viewport — pure CSS, runs on the compositor thread.

02 · animation-range

Per-card windows

animation-range: entry 0% cover 42% means the reveal finishes well before the card reaches center — tune the window live in the debug panel.

03 · @property-ready

Composited line draw

The connector behind the cards uses a second, independent scroll() timeline scaling its height from 0→1 across the whole section.

04 · graceful fallback

IntersectionObserver backup

Browsers without scroll-driven animation support get an equivalent reveal via a tiny observer — same easing, same feel, no missing effect.

05 · reduced motion

Respecting preference

prefers-reduced-motion disables the transform/blur choreography entirely and shows every card at rest.
