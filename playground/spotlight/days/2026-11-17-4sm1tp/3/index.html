Pen

Written, Not Revealed

The honest version of a handwriting animation: a fat pen stroke is clipped to the letter outline, so the ink can only ever land inside the glyph. Each letter gets its own stroke, offset by the stagger, tapered at both ends. Nothing is masked in from the side — type anything and watch it get written.
