Layout That Changes With Zoom

Thousands of cards on one infinite canvas. Far out, each node is a cached thumbnail blitted from an atlas; cross the promote threshold and only what is in view becomes real, typeable DOM. Pan and zoom never touch the DOM at all.

Written, Not Revealed

A fat tapered pen stroke, clipped to the letter outline, so ink can only ever land inside the glyph. Letters are staggered, the nib tapers at both ends, and three pens write the same line at once. Type your own text and watch it get written.

Drag Toward What It Could Become

No drop-zone geometry anywhere in the file. Each card declares the model states it can reach; the engine lays out every one of those futures, reads back where the card lands, and those landings are the drop zones. The board moves with you.

Four Shapes, One Island

Idle, compact, split, expanded — one black pill that never mounts or unmounts. Width, height and radius ride a real spring you can retune; only the content cross-fades. Tap to expand, long-press to dismiss, and watch the settle time.
