Avatar

Six shapes, one pair of eyes

An avatar is a single SVG path in a 100×100 box plus two eye coordinates. Volume is inferred by stacking the same outline as offset cross-sections, so any path you can draw becomes a solid body. The eyes track your pointer; the state drives how the body breathes. Tap one to cycle its mood.

Move the pointer — every avatar on the page looks at it.
