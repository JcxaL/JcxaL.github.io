Spring

Every component runs one spring

Four custom elements, no easing curves anywhere. Each one owns a tiny semi-implicit Euler spring — stiffness, damping, mass — and renders whatever position that spring is at this frame. Interrupt a gesture mid-flight and the velocity carries, because there is no timeline to restart.

<spring-switch>

Switch

Drag it, or just click — the thumb keeps your velocity
Release halfway and the spring decides, carrying your throw.
<spring-accordion>

Accordion

Height is sprung to the measured content box
Why not a transition?
A height transition needs a known end value and restarts from zero when interrupted. A spring just gets a new target and keeps its velocity.
What is being integrated?
Semi-implicit Euler at the display frame rate: force = −k·x − c·v, then v += f/m·dt, then x += v·dt. Nine lines.
When does it stop?
When displacement and velocity both fall under a threshold, the element parks exactly on target and cancels its frame request.
<spring-sheet>

Sheet

Drag down to dismiss — past halfway it leaves
The scrim opacity is driven by the same spring position, so they can never disagree.
<spring-stack>

Card throw

Fling a card; it springs back or flies out

Sprung, not timed

Grab the sheet and throw it. Let go above halfway and it springs back with the velocity you gave it; below, it carries on out. Nothing here reads a duration.
