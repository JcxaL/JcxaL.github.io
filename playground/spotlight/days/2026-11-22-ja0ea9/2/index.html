Registered properties: —

The edge knows where it is

Each scroller carries four gradient mask layers, one per edge. A scroll-driven animation grows the band from nothing as content passes beyond that edge, and clears it the moment you arrive. No JavaScript, no listeners, no wrapper element — the mask lives on the scrolling element itself.

Vertical · prose

Top clear at rest, bottom clear at the end

Scroll this column. The top edge is perfectly sharp until the first line leaves, because there is nothing above to suggest.

As soon as content passes the top, a band grows in — reaching full width after roughly one eighth of the travel.

The bottom works the same way in reverse: it is faded the whole time there is more to read, and it clears exactly when you land at the end.

Because it is a mask and not an overlay, whatever sits behind the scroller shows through the fade. Gradients, images, a video — all of it.

And because the animation is driven by scroll(self y), there is no scroll listener, no resize observer and nothing to throttle.

You have reached the bottom. The lower band is gone.
