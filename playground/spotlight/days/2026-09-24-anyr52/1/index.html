Truchet Weave

Take one asymmetric tile — two opposed quarter-circles, the “Smith tile” — drop it into a grid and rotate each copy at random. The arcs join across every cell boundary, so a pile of identical tiles becomes one continuous maze. Animate the rotations and the maze re-knits itself in front of you.

Inspired by Rombo’s Truchet pattern write-up (after Truchet 1704 / Smith 1987) · UI Spotlight Day 125 · Drop #1
