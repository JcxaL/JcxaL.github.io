Typography · Drop 3

One Selector, Six Levels

Safari 27 shipped :heading: one pseudo-class that matches every heading, plus a functional :heading(1,2) form for individual levels. A whole type hierarchy — three very different documents below — comes from a single block of rules driven by two custom properties.

Editorial

The loom remembers

Before a single thread is tensioned, the weaver has already decided how the cloth will behave: the draft encodes it.

Threading the shafts

Each warp end is assigned to a shaft. That assignment is the grammar of everything that follows.

Tie-up and treadling

The tie-up says which shafts rise together; the treadling says in what order. Multiply them and you have cloth.

A note on floats

Long floats look like satin and snag like satin.
