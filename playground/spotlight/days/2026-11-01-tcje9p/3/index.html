Typography · Drop #3

Text that flows around any shape, not just a float

CSS can wrap text down one side of a float with shape-outside, and that is where it stops: no holes in the middle of a column, no text closing up again underneath. A real editorial engine breaks lines against the free space on each line instead — so a cut-out can sit anywhere, and the paragraph simply flows around it. Drag the three shapes.

Every line is measured, broken and justified in JavaScript against the gaps left by the shapes. The dashed outline is the exclusion the line-breaker actually sees.
