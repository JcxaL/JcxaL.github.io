Each letter gets its own --dx, measured at click time, so the label lands in the bin wherever the pill happens to sit.

The button eats its own label

Destructive actions usually confirm somewhere else — a modal, a toast, a second button. This one keeps the whole transaction inside the control: the word is swallowed, the pill folds into a circle, and a ring spends your undo window in front of you.

Click Delete. Click the circle again before the ring runs out and the letters hop back out of the bin.
