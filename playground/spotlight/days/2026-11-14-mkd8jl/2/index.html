The elbow grid

LCARS has outlived every interface it was drawn against, and the reason is structural: a rail, a sweep, and a rounded corner welding them together. Three numbers — rail width, sweep height, radius — generate the whole language, and border-radius plus one pseudo-element draw the corner.
