Custom Pull-to-Refresh

Native apps fake this with an OS-level bounce; on the web it's hand-built: track the drag distance past scrollTop 0, apply elastic resistance so it feels heavy, spin a rotation tied 1:1 to pull distance, and only fire the refresh once you cross a real threshold.
