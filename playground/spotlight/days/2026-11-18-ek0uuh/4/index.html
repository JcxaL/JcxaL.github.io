Creatures that chase the pointer

Nothing here is keyframed. A chain of joints eases toward the one ahead of it with a bend cap, so the body whips head-to-tail; the feet plant where they land and only lift in diagonal pairs; and the more feet are in the air, the less grip the body has — traction = 0.16 + planted/total × 0.84. Move the pointer over a tile. Leave it alone and it wanders off on its own.

Tuning

ease rules the feel: low is languid, high is snappy. Each joint gets ease − i × 0.06, so the tail always lags.
