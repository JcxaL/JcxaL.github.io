Scroll Rotisserie

Cards spin through 3D space as they cross the viewport, driven by nothing but scrollY and a bit of trig — the classic "3D image rotation on scroll" technique, minus the GSAP dependency.

Symmetric spin

Progress is measured as distance from viewport-center, so the rotation is symmetric on the way in and the way out.

Alternating axis

Odd cards lean on rotateY, even cards lean on rotateX — the axis mix slider blends between them live.

Depth parallax

A subtle translateZ and scale shift rides along with the rotation so the card feels like it's tumbling in space, not just skewing flat.
