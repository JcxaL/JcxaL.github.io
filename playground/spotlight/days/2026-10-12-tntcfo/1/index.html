Scaling that the layout believes in

Chrome 150 made zoom animatable. That matters because zoom is not transform: scale(): zoom changes the box the layout engine measures, so neighbours move, text re-wraps and scrollbars update. A transform only magnifies the painted result after layout has already been decided.

The middle card is zoomed. Because zoom feeds back into layout, the card to its right is pushed along.
