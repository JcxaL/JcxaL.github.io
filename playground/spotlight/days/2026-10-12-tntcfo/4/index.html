A gradient border that is just a background

Gradient borders have always been a workaround — a doubled pseudo-element, a masked wrapper, or border-image giving up your border-radius. Chrome 150's background-clip: border-area clips a normal background to the border box, so one declaration paints the border and the corners keep their curve. Hover any card; the angle is a registered custom property, so it animates.

1 · cards — six at once

Each one is a single element with a conic background clipped to its border area, plus an inner surface.

2 · pills that wake on hover

The animation is paused until the pointer arrives, so a page full of these costs nothing at rest.

3 · thick borders, intact corners

Push the width slider to 10px. The gradient stays inside the border area and the radius survives.

Always spinning

Running regardless of hover, to show the full sweep.

Faster

Only the period differs — same two declarations.

At rest

Static until you hover it.

Without the new value the demo falls back to border-image, which draws the same gradient but squares the corners off — exactly the compromise border-area removes. The badge in the panel tells you which path your browser took.
