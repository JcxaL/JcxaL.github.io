Three components, four behaviours

None of these widgets owns its logic. A popup, a selection, a typeahead and a focus recovery are attached to named slots, and the component is whatever that combination adds up to. Detach a behaviour in the panel and watch the same markup quietly become something simpler — the ARIA it wrote goes with it.
