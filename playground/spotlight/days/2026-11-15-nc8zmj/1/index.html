The camera is an attribute

Every shot below carries one data-move. The move is a @keyframes rule bound to animation-timeline: view(), so scroll position — not elapsed time — drives it, and animation-range decides which slice of the element's journey through the viewport the move occupies. No scroll listener runs anywhere on this page.
