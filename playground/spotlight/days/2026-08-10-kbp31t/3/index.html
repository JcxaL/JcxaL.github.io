Hanging Indent Editorial

Two text-indent keywords went Baseline widely-available in March 2026: hanging and each-line. No JavaScript, no negative-margin hacks — just real paragraph typography the platform now handles natively.

Bibliography — hanging indent

Ihde, Don. Technology and the Lifeworld: From Garden to Earth. Indiana University Press, 1990. The first line sits flush left while every wrapped line after it hangs indented, exactly as library citation styles require.

Latour, Bruno. Reassembling the Social: An Introduction to Actor-Network-Theory. Oxford University Press, 2005. Adjust the slider on the right and every entry on this page reflows together.

Stanza — each-line indent

Line one begins flush, no indent at all, Line two starts hanging after the hard break, but a very long third line that wraps on its own softly continues without a fresh indent at the wrap point, Line four: another hard break, another hang.

Pull-quote — hanging punctuation

Punctuation that hangs outside the measure keeps the left edge of the text block optically straight — the quotation mark sits in the margin instead of eating into the first line.
