Snapping to One Thing

A two-axis snap container picks its snap target per axis. On an irregular board that means the horizontal axis can settle on one card while the vertical axis settles on another, framing the gap between them. Chrome 156 adds scroll-snap-type: pair: snap one axis and the other follows the same element.
