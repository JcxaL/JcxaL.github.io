Reverse From a Standstill

Two Web Animations fixes landed in Firefox 157. reverse() on an animation whose playbackRate is 0 used to be swallowed whole; it now seeks — mirroring currentTime to the other end of the duration and marking the animation playing — so the moment you hand it a non-zero rate it carries on from the mirrored position. And flipping the sign of playbackRate on a scroll-driven animation now mirrors startTime to the opposite end of the timeline instead of stranding it.
