calc-size() Auto-Height Panels

Real <details> elements animating to their true intrinsic height — no JS height measuring, no max-height guesswork. Just calc-size(auto, size) on the native ::details-content box, with name="" making the group mutually exclusive.

Your browser doesn't support calc-size() yet — panels still work, just without the smooth height animation.

Inspired by Chrome for Developers "Animate to height: auto" & the calc-size() accordion pattern · UI Spotlight Day 108 · Drop #2
