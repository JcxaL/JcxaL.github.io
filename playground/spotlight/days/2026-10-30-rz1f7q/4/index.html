Rig

A face built from rays

No sprites and no keyframes: each outline is 128 radii sampled around a circle and lerped toward the next shape by a spring. The pupils are points on a sphere, turned toward your cursor with Rodrigues' rotation and projected flat — so they foreshorten at the edges instead of sliding. Move the pointer, click a face to make it blink, and watch the loop park itself when everything settles.

loop running attribute writes/s 0 springs at rest 0/3 integrator 120 Hz semi-implicit Euler

Only changed attributes are written back to the DOM — the counter above is the real cost of a frame, and it drops to zero the moment the springs stop moving.
