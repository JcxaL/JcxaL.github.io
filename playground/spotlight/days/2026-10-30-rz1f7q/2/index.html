Tokens · third pane

A page with no classes

Three copies of the same semantic markup — not one class attribute between them. The left pane has no stylesheet at all. The middle one adds a classless sheet whose every rule hides inside :where(), so it carries zero specificity. The right one is the same sheet with your tokens on the host — and a plain h2 rule that beats the framework without a single !important.

  weighs nothing, so the page author always wins the cascade by writing ordinary selectors.
