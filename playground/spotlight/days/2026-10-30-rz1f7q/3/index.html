One typeface, four fonts

A composite font is one family assembled from several, each owning a slice of Unicode and each tuned to sit beside the others. @font-face does the assembling: the same font-family name declared four times, split by unicode-range, with size-adjust per slice. Drag a slider and watch one script resize inside an unchanged text node.
