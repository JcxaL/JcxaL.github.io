Auto-Contrast Badges

Five swatches, one CSS function: color: contrast-color(var(--bg)) picks black or white automatically, live, for every background you throw at it — no JS luminance math required (unless it has to be).

Your browser doesn't natively support contrast-color() yet — this demo is running entirely on the JS fallback so it still works.
