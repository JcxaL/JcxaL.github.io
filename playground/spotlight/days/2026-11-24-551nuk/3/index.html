Threads

Each pixel column of the word is one thread. It runs straight through the letter, then bends until it lies flat — the bend is what makes the shape.

The letters are not drawn. They are woven.

A word is scanned column by column. Every column becomes a filament that runs straight where the letter is solid and bends flat where it ends, lit by a sweep travelling along the weave.
