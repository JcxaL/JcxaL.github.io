The engine never moves a box to make room and never routes a line around one. If the result may not read as intended it still draws it, and warns.

The layout is written down, not inferred.

No auto-layout, no force simulation, no springs pulling boxes apart. Each statement says where its box goes relative to another one — right of, below, even with — and the renderer draws exactly that. Edit the file and watch.
