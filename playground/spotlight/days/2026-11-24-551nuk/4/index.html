Aura

Energy is added at a point, projected to the nearest edge, then decays. Typing adds it to a column on the bottom edge and speeds the palette's rotation.

The edge of the window is a surface.

Not a toast, not a spinner. A neon ring hugs the viewport and carries state in its own light — tap anywhere to push energy into the nearest edge, type to light up the bottom, save to send a pulse all the way round.
