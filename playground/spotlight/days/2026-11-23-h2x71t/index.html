Two buffers, a self-spec,
respelling & a playhead

Today's five all replace a recording with a rule. Pixel effects that are pure functions of time, a layout that measures itself off the live DOM, words that respell letter by letter instead of cross-fading, a sheet of elements that answers to one pointer, and three components that render from a frame number you can drag backwards.
