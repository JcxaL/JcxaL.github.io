Hyphens With House Rules

Justified text in a narrow column is only readable if the browser may break words — but left alone it will strand two letters on a line and stack hyphen after hyphen. hyphenate-limit-chars sets the minimum word length and the minimum characters on each side of the break; hyphenate-character decides what the break even looks like.

hyphens:

hyphenate-limit-chars: 6 3 2

Au­thors can place the break them­selves with a soft hy­phen, which works every­where no mat­ter which hy­phen­a­tion dic­tio­nar­ies the brows­er shipped — and hyphenate-character re­styles those breaks too.
