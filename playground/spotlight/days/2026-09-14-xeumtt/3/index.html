Underline Choreography

Nobody needs a pseudo-element to move an underline. text-underline-offset is a real length on the browser's own decoration and it animates, so the line can travel. Chrome 154 adds two more knobs to the same decoration — text-decoration-inset and text-decoration-skip-spaces — which this page switches on wherever your engine has them.

1 · Offset is a distance, not a state

Drag the offset slider. That line is the browser's underline, not a box — it still breaks correctly at descenders and line ends.

2 · Park it low, pull it home

Hover or tab through these — the underline travels up to the baseline rather than fading in. One transitioned property, no wrapper, no ::after.

3 · Bridging the word gaps

Same sentence, one property apart. none keeps a continuous rule; all lifts the line out of every space. New in Chrome 154.

skip-spaces: none

a continuous rule under prose

skip-spaces: all

a continuous rule under prose

⚠ your engine has no text-decoration-skip-spaces yet, so both halves above render identically. The property landed in Chrome 154 beta — the offset choreography in the other sections is unaffected.

4 · Inset, and the marker-pen trick

text-decoration-inset pulls the decoration in from the text's edges; crank thickness and pull the offset negative and the same decoration becomes a highlight that wraps across lines properly.
