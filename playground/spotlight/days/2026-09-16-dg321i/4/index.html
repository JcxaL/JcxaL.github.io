Where scrolling stops

Scroll a list to its end and something has to happen next. auto hands the momentum to the page, contain swallows it, and the new chain value asks for the honest middle: chain to the parent, but never trigger the browser's own boundary actions like pull-to-refresh or back-swipe.

the case it exists for bottom sheet

Drag the grabber to move the sheet, or scroll the list inside it. Switch the list's overscroll-behavior in the toolbar: with contain the sheet stays put once the list is at the top, which is exactly how a native sheet feels.

The page behind the sheet. With auto, scrolling the list past its top starts dragging this instead — the bug every bottom sheet ships with once.
