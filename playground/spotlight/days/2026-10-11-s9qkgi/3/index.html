The spaces you meant to keep

white-space always bundled two unrelated decisions: whether whitespace collapses, and whether lines wrap. white-space-collapse is the first half on its own, with five values — and one of them, break-spaces, behaves like nothing that came before it.

Container width: 100% · each panel below holds the same source text, byte for byte.

white-space-collapse: break-spacesthe new one

Preserves every space and keeps it occupying width at the end of a line — so a wrap can land in the middle of a run of spaces instead of swallowing it.

collapsethe default

Runs of spaces and newlines become a single space. What you typed is a suggestion.

preservelike <pre>

Every space and newline survives; trailing spaces hang past the wrap point rather than taking up room.

preserve-breaksline endings only

Newlines stay, indentation collapses — the right call for user-typed prose in a comment field.

preserve-spacesthe mirror image

Spaces and tabs stay, newlines become spaces — alignment without honouring the author's line breaks.
