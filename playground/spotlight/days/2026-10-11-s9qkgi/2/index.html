Scroll both scenes sideways / downwards after switching. One property replaces the IntersectionObserver you would otherwise write for every floating label.

Labels that know when to leave

Anchor positioning glues a floating element to another element. It does not, by default, tell it when to stop existing: scroll the anchor out of a clipping container and the label stays stranded mid-air. position-visibility is the one-line answer — the anchored element hides itself when its anchor is clipped away, or when it would overflow.
