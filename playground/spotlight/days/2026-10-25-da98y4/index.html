Insets, Hinges, Ink, Peels & Typed Menus

Five patterns from the week the iPhone Duo made hinges a layout problem: a timeline inset that moves when a reveal fires, folding viewport segments, emoji set as ink, a corner that peels like a sticker, and a filterable menu that is really a dialog.
