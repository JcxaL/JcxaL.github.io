A menu that is really a dialog with a search field

Filterable menus usually get built as comboboxes, and then the keyboard model fights the filtering. Base UI's answer: treat it as a dialog. Focus stays in the input the whole time, the highlight moves through aria-activedescendant, every submenu gets its own search field, and focus is never trapped — Tab walks straight out.
