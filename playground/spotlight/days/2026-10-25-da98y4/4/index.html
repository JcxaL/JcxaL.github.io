Micro-interaction · Drop 4

A card that lifts its own corner like a sticker

One length drives the whole gesture. clip-path cuts that much off the card, a triangle of the same size folds back over it, and a layer underneath shows through the hole. Because it is all one custom property, the peel transitions — no canvas, no 3D, no library.

Inspired by Kitty Giraudel — “Folded corner with CSS” · kittygiraudel.com · UI Spotlight Day 141 · Drop #4
