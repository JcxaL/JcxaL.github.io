The same character, set as ink or as a sticker

Dozens of glyphs carry two presentations. For years the only lever was a stray variation selector buried in your copy — U+FE0E for text, U+FE0F for emoji. font-variant-emoji moves the decision into the stylesheet, where a type choice belongs.

Specimen

Four ways to ask

In use

A dispatch set entirely in text presentation — the marks stay monochrome, sit on the baseline, and take the colour of the paragraph around them.

Set a symbol as emoji and it arrives with its own palette, its own vertical metrics, and its own opinion about line height. Set it as text and it behaves like every other glyph in the font: it inherits color, it respects your measure, and it stops shouting. ✈︎ ❄︎ ⚓︎

  • ☎Monochrome marks keep a list legible at small sizes.
  • ✉They inherit the paragraph colour, so dark mode costs nothing.
  • ⚠And they never pull the line box taller than the type around them.
