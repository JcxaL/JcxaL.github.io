The half-second after the click

Most interface jank lives in the moment right after an interaction: a button that resizes when its label becomes Saving…, a list that lurches as a row lands, a drag that stays stuck because the tab lost focus. The fix is not a nicer easing curve — it is reserving every state's space before that state arrives.

The rule underneath all three. Measure the widest, tallest, longest state up front and give it to the element now; then let a spring carry the visible change. Nothing in the layout has to move, so the animation is free to be expressive — and every way a gesture can end (cancelled, blurred, backgrounded, aborted) resolves to a state you designed rather than one you got stuck in.
