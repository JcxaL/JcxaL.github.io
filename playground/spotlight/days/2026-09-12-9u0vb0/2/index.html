Carousels that scroll one way only

Every horizontal carousel on the web is quietly a vertical scroller too. overflow-y: hidden does not remove the scrollport — it just hides the bar, so the box keeps a scroll offset that focus, anchoring and stray gestures can move. Chrome 153 lets you pair a scrollable value with clip: overflow: auto clip scrolls on x and is genuinely finished on y.

Why it is more than tidiness. A box that can scroll on an axis has to be laid out for that axis, so the engine keeps measuring cross-axis overflow you will never show. Declaring the axis dead with clip lets it skip that work — which is exactly the micro-stutter people have been chasing in horizontal carousels and spreadsheet grids. The accessibility win is bigger: focusing an off-screen child can no longer nudge a "hidden" axis and leave your layout half a card out of place.
