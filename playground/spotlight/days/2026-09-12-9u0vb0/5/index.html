Six orbs that say what they're doing

A spinner tells you the machine is busy. An agent doing six different kinds of work deserves six different shapes — searching is not solving, and listening is not weaving. Each of these is a plain 2-D canvas: no WebGL, no CSS filters, one dot-drawing loop and a different way of placing the dots.

Orb

assistant · 64px avatar
Same component, chat size. Inline, at 20px, it is a different design rather than the same one shrunk — fewer dots, fatter dots, slower sweep:
Why canvas and not CSS. Sixty dots with independent depth, alpha and radius would be sixty elements and sixty compositor layers; here they are sixty arc() calls on one bitmap, capped at a device pixel ratio of 2. The loop pauses itself when the orb scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden, and honours prefers-reduced-motion by drawing a single frame — a loading indicator has no business burning a phone battery off-screen.
