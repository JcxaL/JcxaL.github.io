Every card below sets exactly one property: --state. Hover and press change it; the buttons pin it. Descendants match with @container style(--state: …).

One property, five reactions

A style query matches on a custom property of an ancestor. Set --state once on a card and its dot, label, icon, progress bar and footnote all restyle themselves — no class toggling, no inline styles on the children.
