Timing Chart Choreography

One schedule array generates both the <animate> attributes inside three SVG scenes and the Gantt-style chart under them. Chained begin="draw.end" syntax, animateMotion along a path, and a d morph — with speed, pause and restart driven through the SVG timeline itself.

Aura Fields

Four blurred colour bodies per surface, composited with one mix-blend-mode and contained by isolation: isolate, plus an feTurbulence grain on overlay. Swap palette, blend, blur, spread and drift — the generated CSS updates underneath.

Margin Notes

Hand-drawn annotations with no SVG per note: two borders and a rounded corner make the elbow, a conic-gradient wedge makes the arrowhead, attr(data-note) writes the label, and a displacement filter takes the machine-straightness out of both.

Semantic Sound Cues

Ten cues named by meaning — select, confirm, error, purchase, processing — each a Web Audio recipe rendered on the spot, not a file. Three packs change the personality of every control in the demo panel at once, with a live scope and a proper first-gesture unlock.
