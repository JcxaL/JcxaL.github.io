Margin Notes

Inline annotations with no SVG per note and no JavaScript drawing — one span, one attribute.

A review comment shouldn't need a sidebar. Attach it to the exact words it belongs to and the reader never loses the thread. Each note is a curved elbow built from two borders and a rounded corner, with a conic-gradient wedge parked on the end to make the arrowhead. The handwriting comes from the label, pulled straight out of the attribute, and the whole mark is displaced by a tiny turbulence filter so nothing looks machine-straight.

<span class="ann ann-n" data-note="…">text</span> — direction, colour, mark and roughness are all custom properties, so a note restyles without touching the markup.
