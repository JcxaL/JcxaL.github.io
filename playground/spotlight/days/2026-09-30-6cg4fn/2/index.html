Fill the box, margins included

width: 100% has always meant 100% of the parent, and then your margins get added on top and push the box straight out of it. width: stretch means what everyone assumed 100% meant: fill what is actually left. It is the standard spelling of -webkit-fill-available, and it has been quietly shippable for a while.
