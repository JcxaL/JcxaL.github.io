Progress, in one number

Six animations, six different durations, iteration counts, easings and directions. Ask any of them animation.overallProgress and you get the same thing back: how far through the whole performance it is, 0 to 1. Drag Master and every one of them seeks to the same fraction of its own life — not the same millisecond.
