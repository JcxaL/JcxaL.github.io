Cells that settle

Scatter points at random and their Voronoi cells are a mess — slivers next to giants. Lloyd's algorithm fixes it by iterating one deceptively simple move: replace every point with the centroid of its own cell, then rebuild. Repeat and the mosaic relaxes into an even, organic honeycomb. It is the most satisfying loop in computational geometry, and it animates itself.
