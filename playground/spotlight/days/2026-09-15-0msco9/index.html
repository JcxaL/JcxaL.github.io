Five patterns that earn their frames

Today's drops are all about work the browser will happily do badly unless you do it deliberately: letting a mosaic settle instead of scattering it, ramping a blur instead of fading one, reshaping a fallback font so the swap never moves, committing a switch on velocity instead of position, and rendering forty rows instead of a million.
