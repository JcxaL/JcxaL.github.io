Progressive blur

backdrop-filter: —

Drop layers to 1 and the ramp collapses into the flat “blanket” the stack exists to avoid.

Blur with a gradient,
not a blanket

Fading a blurred pane with a mask fades its opacity — the blur radius stays put, so content slides under a hard-edged sheet. Progressive blur ramps the radius itself by stacking thin panes, each blurrier than the last and each masked to its own slice. Apple's chrome does it; the web can too, with about ten divs and no images.

1 · Stacked vs single pane

Identical content, identical peak radius. Scroll both — the left one has an edge you can point at, the right one doesn't.
