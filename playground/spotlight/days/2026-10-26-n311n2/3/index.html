Figures That Hold Still

Four OpenType switches, two CSS properties, no new font file. Tabular figures stop a live number from twitching; old-style figures sit back into prose; real fractions and small caps do the rest.

Figures inside prose

In 1893 the line carried 41,208 passengers between the two harbours; by 1927 the figure had tripled, and the timetable had grown from 6 departures to 38. Lining figures stand as tall as capitals and shout over the sentence — old-style figures have ascenders and descenders, so they settle into the line like lowercase letters.
