Glide, Fillers, Figures, Headroom & Slots

Five patterns from the last few weeks of the platform: grid cells that transition because they're anchored, a grid whose last row never goes ragged, numerals that stop twitching, a hover that spends HDR headroom, and a component that lays itself out from what you slotted into it.

DROP 01animation

Cells That Glide

Grid tracks can't be transitioned. Anchor every tile to its own cell and a reflow turns into an inset change the browser interpolates — interruptible, no view transition, no JavaScript.

No Ragged Last Row

The 1px-gap border grid breaks the moment the final row runs short. Count the empty slots from the resolved column count, fill them — or span one filler across the gap.

Figures That Hold Still

Tabular, old-style, fractions and small caps — four OpenType switches through two CSS properties, with a live detector that tells you whether your font actually has the glyphs.

Brighter Than White

HDR screens keep headroom above page white. dynamic-range-limit hands it to one element on hover, so a badge can light up past every pixel around it — with an SDR fallback.
