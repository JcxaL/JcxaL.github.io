The Host That Reads Its Slots

A component usually waits for an attribute to tell it what it is. With :host:has() it can look at what was handed to it — a badge, a picture, a footer — and lay itself out accordingly.

Three cards, one component, no variant attribute


  
Everything the shell does here is driven by content, not configuration. Slot a
    [slot=media] child and the card goes two-column; slot a badge and the accent rail lights up;
    leave the body empty and it collapses to a compact row. :host(:has(…)) inspects the light
    DOM the component was given, and the newer :host:has(…) looks inside the shadow
    tree the component built for itself.
