UI SPOTLIGHT · DAY 124

Five interfaces that design
the space around the thing

Today's drops all work the margins: the surface a card floats on, the gutter between tiles, the few pixels either side of an underline, the target you have not landed on yet, and the tray a file sits in before it is ever sent. None of them touch the content — they all change how it feels.
