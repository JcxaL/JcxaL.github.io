Multi-column where it started

The original column-rule only ever worked in multi-column layout, which is why designers reached for borders and empty divs everywhere else.

Gap decorations generalise it: any grid or flex container can paint its gutters, and the rules understand where items actually sit.

Because the lines are painted, not laid out, adding them never shifts a single tile — the gap width you designed is the gap width you keep.
