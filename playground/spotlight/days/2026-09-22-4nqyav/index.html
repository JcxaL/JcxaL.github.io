UI SPOTLIGHT · DAY 123

Five interfaces that know
where their edges are

Today's drops all answer the same question from different directions: where does this thing actually end? A sheet of paper folding into itself, a bubble that hugs the shape its text really took, an underline that stops at each word, a subtree switched off without being removed, and a dialog that decides which gestures count as leaving.

Crumple & Release

Three sheets over one creased height field. Drag to paint crumple into the mesh, tap to ball it up or flatten it, and every frame re-shades from the deformed normals — the Houdini-baked effect, rebuilt as plain canvas triangles.

02LAYOUT

Shrink-Wrapped Bubbles

A block box is as wide as its widest allowed line, so balanced text leaves a gap. Name the inline text as an anchor and anchor-size() gives the bubble the size the text actually wrapped to — with a measured fallback you can force on.
