Micro-interaction · an underline with a fundamental

Underlines you can pluck

Cross a link and its underline is plucked like a string: the displacement at the crossing point is decomposed into the first three modes of a fixed-fixed string, each mode oscillating at its own multiple of the fundamental and decaying faster than the one below it. The fundamental is set by the link's width, so a short word rings high and quick and a long phrase wobbles low — the pitch is the typography.

A good footnote behaves like a string: it is fixed at both ends, it carries the weight of the sentence above it, and it answers when you touch it.
