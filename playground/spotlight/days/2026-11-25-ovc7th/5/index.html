Component · a linter that lives in the page

The linter is a component

Most UI bugs only exist once the thing is rendered, so this component measures the rendered result: text runs against their clip boxes, box intersections, hit-tests for occlusion, WCAG 2 contrast against the real background behind each glyph, tap-target minimums and placeholder values. Drag the stress width or swell the copy by 50% and the findings re-measure on the spot. Every pin is clickable.
