Five drops that check their own work

Today's set is about interfaces that hold themselves to a measurement: a rebuilt animation scored frame by frame against its reference, one declaration rendered by four hosts that each answer with their own controls, a headline whose markup says something other than the page, an underline with a real fundamental, and a linter that lives inside the page it is judging. Each demo is standalone, self-contained and reimplemented from the source it credits.
