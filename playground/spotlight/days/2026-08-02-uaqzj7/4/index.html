Reorder Lists with the View Transition API

Drag row 5 to the top the old way and you'd hand-roll a FLIP transform on every sibling. Wrap the DOM mutation in document.startViewTransition() instead, tag each row with a stable view-transition-name, and the browser snapshots old/new positions and animates every displaced row's transform, width and height for you.

Your browser doesn't support document.startViewTransition — reordering below still works, just as an instant jump instead of a morph.
