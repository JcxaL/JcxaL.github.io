CSS initial-letter Drop Caps

Magazine drop caps used to mean a hand-tuned float:left plus a font-size guess and a margin fudge that broke the moment the copy changed. initial-letter: <size> <sink> tells the browser exactly how many lines the capital should span and how far it sinks into the paragraph — laid out correctly, no float, no manual line-height math.

Your browser doesn't fully support initial-letter yet — the first letters below render at normal size instead of as drop caps.
