A handful of small CSS properties that quietly make text easier to read — none of them need a JavaScript text-measuring library to do it.

01 · text-wrap: balance + hanging-punctuation

"The best typography is the kind nobody notices" — until the last line of a headline is one lonely, ugly word

02 · text-wrap: pretty + hyphens + initial-letter

Text-wrap: pretty asks the browser to spend a little more effort on paragraph line-breaking — avoiding a single short word stranded alone on the final line, the kind of "widow" that copy editors have hunted by hand for a century. Combine it with hyphens: auto and long compound words break cleanly at a syllable boundary instead of overflowing or leaving a canyon of whitespace down one side of a narrow column, and an initial-letter drop cap anchors the whole block the way a print magazine would.
