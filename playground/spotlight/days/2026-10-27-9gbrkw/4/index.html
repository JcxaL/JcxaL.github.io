Swipe, And The Menu Is There

Open UI's Declarative Overscroll Actions proposal turns the iOS row-swipe into markup: overscrollcontainer, overscrollarea and a command="toggle-overscroll" button, with the panel inert while it's closed. Until it ships, scroll snapping gets you most of the way — drag a row left, or press its ⋯ button.
