Interest Cards

The team behind Open UI spent two years arguing about what a tooltip really is. The answer they landed on is not a tooltip at all — it is interest: a single declarative signal that fires whether you hovered with a mouse, tabbed to it with a keyboard, or long-pressed it on a phone.

That signal is what the Interest Invoker API standardises. A trigger carries interestfor; the target is any element with the popover attribute. No mouseenter, no timers, no focus traps — and crucially, no bespoke logic that forgets touch users exist.

The delays are CSS, not constants buried in a component: interest-show-delay and its hide counterpart mean a designer can tune the feel in a stylesheet. And because the target is a real popover, it inherits the whole top-layer stack: light dismiss, escape-to-close, correct stacking above everything else on the page.
