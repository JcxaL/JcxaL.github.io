Anchor-Size Spec Panel

Click a card to open its spec sheet. The panel's height is set with CSS anchor-size(height) — it locks to the exact height of the card that opened it, live, with zero JavaScript measuring. Drag a card's ⠿ handle to resize it and watch the panel follow.

Your browser doesn't support anchor-size() yet — showing a centered fallback panel instead. Try latest Chrome or Edge.
