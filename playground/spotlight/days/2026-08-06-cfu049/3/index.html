Spacing That Scales Itself

letter-spacing and word-spacing now accept percentages, scaling proportionally with font-size — no more spacing that looks perfect at 16px and cramped at 64px.

Fixed em (old way)
SIGNAL & NOISE
letter-spacing: 0.02em — spacing ratio never changes when you resize.
Percentage (new)
SIGNAL & NOISE
letter-spacing: 2% — the same visual proportion at every size.
text-justify comparison

The browser — not JavaScript — now distributes the extra space in a justified line. Inter-word spreads whitespace between words like a classic newspaper column, while inter-character spaces every glyph evenly, which suits dense scripts and display headlines alike.
