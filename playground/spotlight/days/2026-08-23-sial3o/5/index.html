Drop #5 · Component

Viscous ring carousel

A carousel rendered as one fullscreen WebGL fragment shader evaluating signed-distance fields. Neighbouring cards melt into each other as they close up, and separate cleanly once the smooth-min radius no longer reaches between them. Drag to spin, click a card to snap it to the front.
