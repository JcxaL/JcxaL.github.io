Scroll-Aware Glass Dock

Scroll down and the floating dock shrinks into a compact pill, hiding its breathing room. Scroll back up and it expands again. A sliding highlight glides between tabs, and the page's edge blurs progressively as content passes beneath the dock.

↓ keep scrolling to see the dock react ↓

The mechanism mirrors a mobile "liquid glass" tab bar: track scroll velocity/direction, toggle a compact state class, and let CSS transitions do the shrinking. No animation library required.
