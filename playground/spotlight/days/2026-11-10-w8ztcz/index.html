Long lenses, declared planes, bars that read as letters

Today's five all hide their machinery somewhere unexpected: in a focal length, in an attribute selector, in the character codes of a string, in a spring constant, and in a stack of generated keyframes.

The Lens Opens

Three buildings drawn as flat elevations are really 3D models seen down an 800mm lens. Scroll and the lens shortens to 24mm — the drawing becomes the building without a single new vertex.

Isometric by Declaration

No canvas, no library: each face carries data-iso="top|front|side" and the stylesheet supplies the one rotation that plane needs. Rotation, tilt, grid unit and explode are four custom properties.

Type That Is the Data

A face whose every glyph is a bar and whose every character is a value — so a waveform is a string you can select, edit and restyle. Three variation axes change width, roundness and alignment.
