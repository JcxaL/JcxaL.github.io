Every pixel is paired, then tweened

Two pixel sprites, one box-shadow. Each lit pixel of the first sprite is matched to a pixel of the second, the pair is interpolated over a handful of frames, and the whole flight is written out as a @keyframes rule. No canvas, no JS at playback — the browser animates a shadow list.
