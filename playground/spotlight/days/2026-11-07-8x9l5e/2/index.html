Greedy flex-wrap: wrap fills each line to the brim and leaves whatever is left alone on the last row. flex-wrap: balance keeps the same number of lines and spreads the items evenly across them — and flex-line-count sets a floor on how many lines there must be. Drag the width and watch the last row stop being an orphan.
