One toast that morphs in place

Most toast stacks pile up. This one reuses the toast already on screen: its box springs from one size to the next while the text swaps, so loading → success is a single object changing its mind. Size, not opacity, is what animates — and because it's a spring, interrupting mid-morph keeps the velocity instead of restarting.
