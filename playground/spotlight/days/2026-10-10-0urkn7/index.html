Five things the browser already does for you

Today's five are all jobs the platform quietly took over: three animations on one element without a transform matrix, an :nth-child() count that survives filtering, Japanese digits that stand upright on their own, a text caret that stops blinking on command, and a suggestion UI that four different form controls have been able to draw for a decade.
