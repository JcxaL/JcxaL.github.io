Tune the render

Live ASCII renderer

Three procedural scenes, none of them images: each frame is sampled to a luminance grid, and every cell picks a glyph from a brightness ramp. Nothing is drawn — the whole animation is text in a <pre>, which is why it costs almost nothing and scales to any character set you hand it.

Torus —

      
Z-buffered donut. Points on a torus are rotated, projected, and shaded by the dot product of the surface normal with a fixed light.

    

    

      
Plasma —

      

      
Summed sine fields. Four travelling waves are added per cell; the result maps straight onto the ramp with no geometry at all.

    

    

      
Sea —

      

      
Height field. Layered swells give each column a surface height; cells above it stay empty, cells below shade by depth and foam.
