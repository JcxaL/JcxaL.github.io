Scroll energy

Scroll speed scramble

Nothing here animates on a timeline. A frame loop watches how far the page moved since the last frame, smooths that into an energy value, and each character rolls the dice against it. Flick the page and the headlines shred; let go and they resolve one glyph at a time, because the energy decays rather than snapping back.
