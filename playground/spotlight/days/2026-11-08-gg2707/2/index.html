Corner geometry

Native frame shapes

A chamfer on one vertex, a notch on another, a scoop on a third — and a stroke that follows all of them. Each panel is a single clip-path: shape() path generated per corner, drawn twice: once at full size in the stroke colour, once inset by the stroke width in the surface colour. That second pass is what gives a shaped box a real border instead of a painted-on outline.

Your browser doesn't support clip-path: shape() — panels fall back to plain rounded corners.

The declarative cousin — corner-shape

corner-shape reshapes whatever border-radius already describes, so it keeps real borders and shadows for free — but it applies one family to the whole box. Generating shape() per vertex is the escape hatch when you want a bevel at the top-left and a scoop at the bottom-right of the same panel.
