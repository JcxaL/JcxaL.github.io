Overlap, Detected

CSS can't ask "are these two boxes about to collide?" — so this borrows a trick: anchor positioning stretches an invisible strip between the right edge of one element and the left edge of the other, and parks a fixed-width child inside it. The moment the gap gets smaller than that child, the strip overflows, its scroll timeline goes live, and a style container query reads the flag. Drag a slider and watch the decoration get out of the way.
