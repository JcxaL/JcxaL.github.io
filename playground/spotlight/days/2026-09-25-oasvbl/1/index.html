Every Mark Is a Function

Nothing here is a drawing. Each stroke is a function of a seed: the wobble, the overshoot past the corner, the pressure taper and the second pass that never quite retraces the first. Feed the same seed and you get the same picture on every machine — bump the seed a few times a second and you get boil, the twitch that makes hand-drawn animation feel alive.
