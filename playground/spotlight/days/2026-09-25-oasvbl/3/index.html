Split It Properly

Every per-letter text animation starts with splitting the string, and almost every one of them is wrong. [...text] tears a family emoji into five people, strands Devanagari vowel signs and detaches combining accents. Intl.Segmenter — in every browser since 2024 — splits the way a reader would: by grapheme, by word (even where there are no spaces) and by sentence.

Words, without spaces

Japanese and Thai don't separate words with spaces. split(' ') returns the whole line; the word segmenter returns actual words.

Sentences that aren't just dots

A reading highlighter that moves sentence by sentence. split('.') never sees a question or an exclamation, and it cuts decimals in half.
