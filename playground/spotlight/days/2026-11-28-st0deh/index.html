Five drops where the platform does the work

Today's set leans on things the browser itself grew recently: randomness resolved by the style engine, a displacement filter running inside backdrop-filter, and a native <select> you are finally allowed to style. The other two are craft rather than spec — motion built for text that arrives in tokens, and a dial that earns its detents with a spring. All five are clean-room rewrites in one self-contained file each.

DROP 01Animation

The Random Is in the Stylesheet

Ninety sparkles, forty-four equaliser bars and eighty confetti chips get every coordinate, hue and duration from CSS random() — no loop writes an inline style. The last strip shows the one gotcha: pass a --dashed-ident cache key and the whole row rolls once instead of once each.

DROP 02Layout

Glass That Bends the Page Behind It

Frosted glass only blurs. Each tile here bakes a displacement map from its own rounded-rect distance field — neutral centre, steep lip at the rim — and runs it as feDisplacementMap inside backdrop-filter. Drag the pill across the headline and watch the letters fold.

DROP 03Typography

Text That Arrives, Not Text That Appears

Three panels stream the same copy under three policies: words that settle as they land, a paced mask that speeds up when the buffer runs ahead, and a review-bound rewrite that holds its word-level diff until you accept or discard it.

DROP 04Micro-interaction

A Dial That Clicks Into Place

Angle stays continuous while you drag; a spring pulls toward the nearest notch and the momentum you let go with carries you past a few more. Every notch crossed fires a tick — ring flash, and a real vibration where the device has one.

Inspired by the Detent Dial in guanmo-ai/ui-moments

DROP 05Component

The Native Select, Finally Styleable

One <select>, three pickers: a grid of cards, a colour swatch matrix and a staggered list. appearance: base-select hands over the rendering while typeahead, focus and the form value stay exactly where the platform left them.
