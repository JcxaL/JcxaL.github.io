Detent physics

Snap is a spring toward the nearest notch, applied every frame — the dial still has momentum between notches, so a fast flick rolls past several before settling.

A dial that clicks into place

A slider tells you nothing on the way past. A detented dial does: angle is continuous while you drag, but a spring pulls toward the nearest notch, momentum carries you past a few on a flick, and each notch crossed fires a tick — a ring flash, and a real navigator.vibrate where the device has one. Three dials, three detent counts: fine, stepped, and free with soft notches.
