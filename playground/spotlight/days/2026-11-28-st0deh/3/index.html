Motion policy

Text that arrives, not text that appears

Generated text does not land as a finished paragraph — it arrives in tokens, at an uneven rate, and sometimes it rewrites what it already wrote. Three patterns handle that: words that settle as they land, a paced reveal that catchUps when the buffer runs ahead, and a review-bound rewrite that holds its diff until you accept it. Each panel streams the same copy under a different policy.
