Glass that bends the page behind it

Frosted glass only blurs. Real glass moves what is behind it. Each tile here runs an SVG feDisplacementMap inside backdrop-filter, fed by a displacement map baked in a canvas from the tile's own rounded-rect distance field: neutral in the middle, a steep lip at the rim that drags the backdrop outward. Drag the pill over the headline to watch it fold.
