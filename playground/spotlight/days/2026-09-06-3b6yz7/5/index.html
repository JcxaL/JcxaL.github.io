Snap Sheet

A bottom sheet that is still a native <dialog> — focus trap, Esc, inert page and ::backdrop for free — with pointer dragging, snap points and velocity dismissal layered on top.

The grip owns the gesture with touch-action:none; the body keeps native scrolling and overscroll-behavior:contain, so a sheet never drags the page behind it.
