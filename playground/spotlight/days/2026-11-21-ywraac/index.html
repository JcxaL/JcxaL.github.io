Bones, artboards, punctuation, caramel & boiling ink

Five patterns that let a rule do the drawing: a mesh bound to bones so the art follows the skeleton, an artboard whose layers are real HTML and whose edits land in a shared class, a paragraph that corrects its own punctuation pass by pass, a selection that flows between buttons like syrup, and components whose outlines are redrawn, crookedly, several times a second.
