Phoneme inventory

Tap a sound to add it. Tap again to make it frequent (3× weight). A third tap removes it.

Consonant classes available to syllable templates and rules: C any consonant · P plosive · N nasal · S fricative · L liquid · G glide · V any vowel

Syllable shapes

Templates are drawn by weight. CV is a plain open syllable; CLVN wants a consonant, a liquid, a vowel and a nasal.

Word length

A slot whose class has no sounds in the inventory is quietly skipped, so a template can never dead-end.

Sound change rules

Applied top to bottom, each one across the whole word at once — the way a real sound law sweeps a language.

Syntax from > to / before_after. Use ∅ or - to delete, # for a word edge, a class letter (V, C, N…) or a literal sound for context. Leave the environment off to apply everywhere.
Examples: p > f / V_V · a > e / _# · s > ∅ / _C · t > d / N_

Romanisation
