Recipes

  • Drag with the Belt tool — belts auto-orient along the path you draw, corners included.
  • Mines emit ore into the tile they face. Smelters turn 2 ore into 1 plate, Assemblers turn 1 copper + 1 iron plate into a circuit.
  • Splitters round-robin their output across the three tiles that aren't behind them. Shipping counts everything that arrives.
  • Belts refuse anything arriving through the face they output from, so two belts can't fight head-on.
  • 1–9 pick a tool, R turns the facing, Space runs/pauses, Backspace is the eraser.
  • Share puts the whole blueprint in the URL. JSON downloads it, Import reads it back, PNG saves a picture of the floor.
  • Your factory and finished contracts persist in this browser automatically.
