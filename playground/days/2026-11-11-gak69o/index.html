How it flies

Gravity is the real thing — an inverse-square pull from Terra, plus a nudge from the moonlet Selene. Below 190 km the air bites and orbits decay; touch the surface and you are a crater. Drag in empty sky to fling a new satellite.

Impulsive manoeuvre

Green dashes preview the orbit you will be on. Hold W/S for live prograde thrust, A/D for radial.

Transfer solver

Δv₁ burns at your current radius to raise the far side; coast to apoapsis, then Δv₂ rounds it off. Budget left is shown on the Fleet tab.
