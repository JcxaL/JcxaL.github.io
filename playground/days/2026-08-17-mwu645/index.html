How to Play

Each turn you gain +1 max mana (up to 10) and draw a card. Play creatures and spells from your hand by tapping them — some spells need a target, tap the enemy hero or a creature to aim. Tap one of your creatures (glowing gold) to attack — it can hit the enemy hero or any enemy creature. Reduce the enemy hero to 0 HP to win.
