Build a machine out of bars

  • Joint drops a pivot, Bar links two pivots into a rigid rod.
  • Ground bolts a pivot to the bench; Crank makes a grounded bar spin the whole rig.
  • Coupler welds a point onto a bar — that's where the interesting curves live.
  • Trace pins a pen to any joint. Move drags joints, drag the bench to pan, wheel to zoom.
  • Rockers that can't complete a turn reverse automatically at their limits.
